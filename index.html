Services

What we manage.

Four parts of the process, run end to end so you're not managing dozens of separate conversations.

i.

Creator Sourcing

We find and vet creators who match the campaign — niche, geography, audience, content quality and brand fit.

ii.

Outreach & Negotiation

We contact creators, manage follow-ups and availability, and agree on rates, requirements and deliverables.

iii.

Campaign Coordination

We run product seeding and paid collaborations from creator selection through to published content.

iv.

Tracking & Reporting

We track participation, deadlines and deliverables, then organize the results into a clear report.
